Delete the quiet moments#

A persistence forecast says the next price will equal the last one. On an ordinary tick grid, it is correct whenever the price stays flat. Delete every repeated price and persistence becomes wrong at every remaining step by construction.

What is mathematical
After deduplication, every remaining transition satisfies pₜ ≠ pₜ₋₁. A no-change forecast therefore has exactly 0% direction accuracy.
What the paper measured
On a matched population, the same preprocessing inflated persistence RMSE by 27.4%. This toy sequence demonstrates the mechanism, not that empirical estimate.

Hold accuracy constant#

Evaluation is part of the model#

A benchmark decides what counts as skill. A metric decides what kinds of mistakes are visible. In both papers, changing the evaluation changed the scientific conclusion without changing the forecast.
