A Lean Formalization of the Marchenko–Pastur Law for Independent Signs

Arya Somu · Lean formalization

A Lean 4 proof of the Marchenko–Pastur law for sample covariance matrices with independent fair-sign entries. The development connects exact walk counts and ordered rooted trees to moment concentration, proves normalization and all moments of the density plus its atom at zero, and concludes weak convergence in probability. Covers every positive aspect ratio, including one.

Sharp Variance Bounds for Random Greedy Independent Sets in Split Graphs and Cographs

Arya Somu · Proposition · Draft

Exact maximum variance bounds for random-order greedy independent-set size on split graphs and cographs, with a unique extremizer in each class. The draft also gives a stability result and a counterexample for general graphs. Independent proof review and a broader prior-art assessment remain open.

Five Days Are Enough: A Sharp Finite-Sample Gap in Calibrated Forecast Aggregation

Arya Somu · Research note · Draft

A five-observation counterexample showing that replacing binary outcomes with conditional means can change a calibrated forecast-aggregation problem. Three experts force every outcome, while the relaxation guarantees only three correct predictions. The note proves minimality and extends the result to calibration error and repeated observations.

A Hidden Arrow of Time in Fair-Looking Coin Flips

Arya Somu · Research paper · Draft

A stationary process can have exactly fair 20-flip windows and nearly one bit of entropy per flip, yet an arbitrarily large time-reversal divergence rate. The draft derives the sharp entropy threshold and optimal concentration of bias, then certifies discontinuous changes in the optimizer for fair and 40/60 coins. Proofs and exact checks are included; independent review and novelty assessment remain open.

On the Additive Composition of Two and Two

Arya Somu · Mathematical humor

A 22-page proof that 2 + 2 = 4 using category theory, algebraic K-theory, Hodge theory, and heat kernels. Includes six appendices and a fictional referee exchange. The result also agrees with counting.
