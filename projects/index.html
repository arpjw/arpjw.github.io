Projects

Experiments, tools, and things I’ve built.

Post-training

  • nemotron - Nemotron Mini 4B post-training for banking intents and graph repair.
  • rna - Qwen3-8B adapted for RNA inverse folding with supervised training and RL.
  • reward-function - Audited Qwen3-4B reward experiments; no demonstrated practical token savings.
  • inkling - A Brier-score RL pilot for poker probabilities; calibration gains remained inconclusive.

Kernels & compilers

  • trainkernel - Compiles a supported static PyTorch training step into one cooperative CUDA kernel.
  • tinynpu - A verified 4×4 INT8 systolic array in SystemVerilog with an interactive RTL trace viewer.
  • yolk - An OCaml e-graph engine with relational matching, extraction, and proof explanations.
  • minos - Hindley–Milner type inference with row polymorphism and a browser playground.

Learning dynamics

  • grokking - Replicating delayed generalization in a small transformer trained on modular arithmetic.
  • spl - Sparse parity learning with an MLP and a Fourier-based measure of training progress.

Finance

  • exchange-in-a-box - A deterministic OCaml market simulator with matching, replay, risk controls, and synthetic agents.
  • skew - A typed financial-contract DSL with Monte Carlo pricing, binomial lattices, and autodiff Greeks.
  • adverse-selection-engine - Rolling bid-ask spread estimates from Binance trades using three microstructure models.
  • velocity - Shadow-mode research on prediction-market moves and related equities; live execution disabled.
  • prism - A connector format and registry for prediction-market, economic, and news time series.
  • crucible - An adversarial agent framework for reviewing fund research, risk, and portfolio decisions.
  • pbl-markets - A chapter prediction market for De Anza PBL, using virtual points.
  • favorable-bet - A reproducible simulation of expected wealth, typical outcomes, and fractional betting.

Forecasting

  • causal-interventions - An exact Bayes-risk benchmark for experiment choice by open language models.
  • shared-evidence - Synthetic studies of forecast aggregation when forecasters share information.
  • credible-intervals - Checking Bayesian interval coverage when the fitted likelihood has the wrong variance.

Math & games

  • matrices - A Lean 4 formalization of the Marchenko–Pastur law for independent fair-sign matrices.
  • traffic - An executable essay with four traffic controllers and an exhaustive finite-state checker.
  • regret - A Rust CFR+ solver for heads-up poker subgames, with a convergence dashboard and range explorer.

Onchain

  • xenith - Transport-agnostic cross-chain state synchronization in Rust, with explicit divergence handling.
  • auric - An educational ERC-20, vesting, and constant-product AMM stack, with a Sepolia testnet interface.
  • crowdfunder - Milestone crowdfunding with contributor votes and campaign escrows on Base Sepolia.

Tools & experiments

  • aegis - A coding-agent plugin for smart-contract review, gas analysis, and DeFi development.
