Judgement and calibration are the bottlenecks to good decisions under uncertainty. Models are already capable of the former and remain unreliable at the latter. Laplace Research is our attempt at solving the latter, so that human judgement can be spent choosing which questions matter rather than second-guessing the answers.

I study CS and Math at the University of California, San Diego, and research rare events at Stanford's AFT Lab. I'm broadly interested in continual learning, the role AI will play in exchange operations, and thinking & interleaved reasoning mid-training.

Previously, I interned on Esplanade Capital's fixed income desk, where I worked on prepayment modeling and OAS analysis. Before this, I did research on subatomic forces, managed a 1000+ transaction resale business, and tried competing in competitive math, physics, and programming. When I'm free, I'm typically reading technical reports or forecasting literature, playing very competitive poker, lifting, or playing badminton.
