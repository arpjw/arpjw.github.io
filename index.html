Arya Somu

Judgement and calibration are the bottlenecks to good decisions under uncertainty. Models can now produce forecasts at scale, but not ones whose confidence you can take at face value. What we're building at Laplace Research is our attempt at solving the latter, so that human judgement can be spent choosing which questions matter rather than second-guessing the answers.

I am the founder and Chief Investment Officer of Monolith Investments LP, an AI-influenced Infrastructure and Macro investor. I study CS and Math at the University of California, San Diego and research rare events at Stanford's AFT Lab. I'm broadly interested in continual learning, exchange operations, and frontier intelligence overall.

Previously, I interned on Esplanade Capital's fixed income desk, where I worked on prepayment modeling and OAS analysis. I also interned at Alexandria, where I built agents across trading and security. Before this, I did research on subatomic forces, managed a very high-end resale business specializing in rare and one-off sneakers, and tried competing in competitive math, physics, and programming. When I'm free, I'm typically reading technical reports or forecasting literature, playing very competitive poker, lifting, or playing badminton.
