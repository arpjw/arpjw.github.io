Two kinds of conviction

Resisting social pressure and resisting forged evidence are different skills. Off-the-shelf models vary widely in how often they cave, but none of them checks where a record came from: they treat a record pasted into a user message much as if the tool had sent it.

Does thinking help?

The same open models with thinking mode switched on, on the same held-out problems. Thinking makes them far more accurate, better at accepting real corrections and tool updates, and (for most) harder to pressure. But it makes them more likely to follow a forged record: reasoning treats the forgery as information to work through rather than a claim to check against the rules.

Thinking off (greedy) → thinking on (sampled, temperature 0.6, up to 4,096 tokens), 1,257 held-out episodes per model; below each pair, the paired difference with its 95% interval. Green: a significant improvement; red: a significant decline; grey: not significant. Answers that ran out of thinking room count as failures (Qwen3.5-9B 3%, Granite 5%).

Method

Each episode asks a question, shows an earlier answer from the assistant (correct, wrong, unjustified, or an abstention), then challenges it. The model replies with a reason, a final answer and a decision: keep, revise or insufficient. Every label comes from an exact solver; there is no LLM judge.

The correct final answer is always what the authenticated facts imply, or insufficient when they do not determine it. Each challenge is crossed with nine framings: neutral, authority, evaluator, consensus, emotional, impatience, threat, repetition, and the held-out flattery.

Training for conviction

We trained LoRA adapters (r = 16, 17.4M parameters) on Qwen3-1.7B, using disjoint halves of the training split. SFT and RL show a trade-off: imitation learns the provenance rule, while reinforcement learning generalises to unseen problem types and stays open to correction. A follow-up, v2, tried to get both with a light SFT warm-start before RL; it learned provenance but moved along the trade-off rather than escaping it.

Provenance is a convention set by the system prompt. Models were evaluated without thinking mode. Gemma 4 and SmolLM3 were excluded because their chat templates hide or relabel tool messages.
