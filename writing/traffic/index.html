What does this check assume?

Two incompatible through movements; atomic state changes; a clock that keeps ticking; lamps that exactly follow the model. It checks simultaneous green and recurring green service. It does not model cars inside the crossing, turning traffic, pedestrians, hardware, or collisions. A green opportunity does not mean every queued vehicle gets through.
