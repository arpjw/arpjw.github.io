Choosing Experiments for Causal Prediction

Arya Somu · 2026

An exact Bayes-risk benchmark asks whether language models can choose the experiment that best improves a future causal prediction. Locked studies find that unaided 32B, 72B, and 235B conditions did not meet their registered better-than-random rules. A 32B agent improved with an exact-risk calculator, though its tool use was incomplete. The registered v5 235B comparison was not run.

Climate Information and Wheat Futures: A Filtered-Information Theory of Forecast Value, Equilibrium Response, and Trading Feasibility

Arya Somu · 2025

Develops a mathematical theory of climate information in wheat futures, with proofs for physical forecast value, equilibrium price sensitivity, the nonidentification of trading alpha, and cost-aware abstention. Includes full derivations and a point-in-time testing protocol; no completed backtest or profitability claim.

ConvictionBench: Evidence-Sensitive Belief Revision in Language Models

Arya Somu and Bruce Nshuti Hirwa · Laplace Research edition · November 2026

An oracle-labeled benchmark tests when language models keep an answer, accept a correction, or express uncertainty. A controlled comparison found that the trained adapters relied on how records were worded when judging whether to accept them.
