Choosing Experiments for Causal Prediction

Arya Somu · 2026

An exact Bayes-risk benchmark asks whether language models can choose the experiment that best improves a future causal prediction. Locked studies find that unaided 32B, 72B, and 235B conditions did not meet their registered better-than-random rules. A 32B agent improved with an exact-risk calculator, though its tool use was incomplete. The registered v5 235B comparison was not run.
