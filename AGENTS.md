# Writing reference

Before creating or publishing an article, read `writing/_article-preview.html`,
`writing/post-template.txt`, and the Writing section of `README.md`.

Use the sample to match the article's structure and the shared `styles.css` rules
for fonts, content width, and spacing. Choose an accent color for each article;
keep both light and dark modes readable.

The underscore-prefixed sample is a repository reference. Do not rename it to
a public HTML path or add it to `writing/index.html`. List only articles Arya
has approved for publication.

Keep the navigation's Writing link pointed at `/writing/`. Keep the homepage
Substack button pointed at Arya's Substack. The Writing index footer uses the
RSS feed instead.

Display Roman-numeral volume labels instead of publication dates on the Writing
index and in every article header. Keep existing volume assignments fixed and
assign the next unused volume after pulling the latest index. Use the same label
on both surfaces. Keep actual publication timestamps in RSS and factual dates
in research text and citations.
