# Writing reference

Before creating or publishing an article, read `writing/_article-preview.html`,
`writing/post-template.txt`, and the Writing section of `README.md`.

Use the sample to match the article's structure and the shared `styles.css` rules
for fonts, content width, and spacing. Choose an accent color for each article
with enough contrast against the light background. The site uses light mode only.

The underscore-prefixed sample is a repository reference. Do not rename it to
a public HTML path or add it to `writing/index.html`. List only articles Arya
has approved for publication.

The homepage is the entry point for site sections and social profiles. On other
pages, use a single `← Home` link to `/` above the content. Omit site-wide
navigation bars, footers, and social profile links. Keep useful in-page tables of
contents and links to sources. Link to the RSS feed from the Writing index.

Display Roman-numeral volume labels instead of publication dates on the Writing
index and in every article header. Volumes number the published essays
consecutively in publication order, starting at Volume I. Give a new article
the next number after the highest volume in the latest index. If an article is
removed, renumber the remaining ones so the sequence has no gaps. Use the same
label on both surfaces. Keep actual publication timestamps in RSS and factual dates
in research text and citations.
